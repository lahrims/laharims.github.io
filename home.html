About Me

Machine Learning & Autonomy Engineer

I'm Lahari, and I like building things that make robots a little less confused.

Most of my work is around perception, autonomy, and machine learning: radar tracking, computer vision, sensor fusion, path planning, and getting models to actually run on edge devices instead of just looking good in a notebook.

I like messy problems: noisy sensors, weird edge cases, real-world data, and systems that need to make safe decisions anyway.

This portfolio is a collection of the projects I've worked on, the things I've built, and the problems that made me stare at my screen a little too long.

When I'm not debugging something, I'm probably solving puzzles, reading fantasy, or stargazing.
